# Jainam Broking Limited - Company Report

A company analysis report prepared during a summer internship at Jainam Broking Limited (June–September 2025), examining the firm's departmental structure, business operations, and strategic initiatives across the financial services sector.

**Co-authored with:** Khushi Jariwala and Jal Shah

📄 **[Read the full report →](Jainam_Broking_Company_Report.pdf)**

## Scope

The report is structured across 8 chapters:

1. **Industrial / Sectoral Scenario** - history and evolution of India's financial services sector, from early banking through nationalization, market reforms, and the current fintech/digital transformation era.
2. **Company Profile** - Jainam Broking Limited's history, founding, branch network, and subsidiaries.
3. **Finance Department** - structure and function.
4. **Marketing & Sales Department** - structure and function.
5. **Human Resource Department** - recruitment process, performance appraisal system, employee policies.
6. **Risk Management & Surveillance Department** - structure and function.
7. **Wealth Guardian Department** - structure and function.
8. **Conclusion.**

## Context

Prepared as part of the Bachelor of Management Studies (International Business) program, Bhagwan Mahavir University, based on direct observation and internal briefings during the internship period.
