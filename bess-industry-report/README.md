## Overview

This report analyzes the Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) sector as an investment opportunity, combining policy analysis, technology assessment, and financial modeling to evaluate India's energy storage buildout against global benchmarks. It was built using the same due diligence framework applied to private investment deals during the internship - market sizing, regulatory risk mapping, unit economics, and competitive positioning - rather than a purely academic literature summary.

## Key Findings

- **Market size:** India's BESS market was valued at $7.8B (₹67,080 Cr) in 2024, against a global market of $37.5B (₹3,22,500 Cr)
- **Growth trajectory:** India's BESS capacity is projected to grow from 34.7 GWh (FY26-27) to 236.2 GWh (FY31-32) - a ~47.5% CAGR, per CEA projections
- **Falling costs:** Co-located BESS tariffs have fallen from ₹10 lakh/MW-month to ₹2.5 lakh, undercutting gas peaking plants
- **Structural risk:** ~90% of Li ion cells are still imported, and the entire LFP/NMC installed base carries multi year obsolescence risk from emerging Na ion and solid state chemistries
- **Revenue model insight:** Merchant participation in power exchanges (DAM + ancillary services) is outperforming tender-only tariff contracts - a finding that shapes the report's core investment recommendation
